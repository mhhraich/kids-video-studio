🎬 Kids Video Studio

A private tool that creates short educational videos for young children.

What it does

Kids Video Studio turns a simple idea (a short story, good manners, colors, numbers, letters...) into an animated video with friendly characters, narration and subtitles, in several languages including Arabic, French and English.

The owner of the tool can connect their own YouTube channel so the finished videos are uploaded to that channel, marked as “Made for kids”. Each video is uploaded as private first so it can be reviewed before it is published.

Who uses it

This tool is used only by the channel owner to manage their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from viewers or other people.
