Privacy Policy

Kids Video Studio · Last updated: 26 October 2026

Kids Video Studio (“the app”) is a private tool used by a single YouTube channel owner to create educational videos for children and upload them to their own channel. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

1. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Data the app accesses

  • Your Google sign-in token: when the channel owner connects their account, Google gives the app a token that allows it to act on that YouTube channel.
  • Channel name: read once to show which channel is connected.
  • Public video search results and view counts: used to find popular topics for children's videos. Only public information is read.

The app does not access any data about viewers, children, subscribers or other users, and does not collect personal information from anyone other than the channel owner.

3. How the data is used

  • To upload videos created by the app to the owner's own channel, with title, description, tags, thumbnail and the “Made for kids” setting.
  • To choose video topics based on public search trends.

The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with any third party.

4. Storage and security

The sign-in token and the videos are stored only on the owner's private server, protected by a password. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube and the services used to create the video content (text-to-speech and script writing), which receive only the video text, never account data.

5. Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can disconnect YouTube at any time from the app, which deletes the stored token. Access can also be revoked from the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder removes all stored data.

6. Children

The videos are made for children, but the app itself is used only by an adult channel owner. The app does not collect any information from children. Videos are marked “Made for kids” on YouTube so that YouTube applies its children's privacy protections.
